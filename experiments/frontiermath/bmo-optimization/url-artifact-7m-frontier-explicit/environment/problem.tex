Let
\[
c=\sup_f\int_0^1\left(f(t)^3+|f(t)|\right)\,dt
\]
where the supremum is over all integrable functions
\(f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R\) satisfying
\[
\int_0^1 f(t)\,dt=-10,\qquad
\int_0^1 f(t)^2\,dt=100+\frac1{12},
\]
and
\[
\sup_{J\subset[0,1]}\frac1{|J|}\int_J
\left|f(t)-\frac1{|J|}\int_J f(s)\,ds\right|^2dt
\leq\frac1{12},
\]
where the supremum is over all subintervals \(J\subset[0,1]\).

Find \(c+\frac{1985}{2}\).
